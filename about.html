About me

A first-year student who is still at the beginning, and happy to be.

I am a first-year BSIT student specializing in Game Development. I am still new to programming and web development, so this website is one of the first larger things I have built from scratch. I would rather be honest about where I am than pretend to be further along.

Outside of class I spend a lot of time with games, technology, and anything with a good story behind it. Cars and cityscapes catch my eye, and I take photos and draw whenever I get the chance. I enjoy creative projects where everything comes together: a game with a strong atmosphere, a photo with good light, or a layout that just feels right.

I like learning by trying things out, and I would rather learn in public than wait until I feel ready. I am curious, I like figuring out how things work, and I try to keep improving even when a project does not go the way I planned.

Where I am headed

My goal is to get good enough at game development to build small games I am proud of, and to keep my art and photography going alongside the code. Every project here is a step toward that.
